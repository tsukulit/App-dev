# TOKYO GHOUL 🩸

## About My Favorite Anime

**Tokyo Ghoul** is a dark fantasy and supernatural horror anime created by *Sui Ishida*. The story takes place in an alternate Tokyo where humans and ghouls secretly live together. Ghouls look like ordinary people but survive by feeding on human flesh.

## Story Overview

The story follows **Ken Kaneki**, an ordinary college student whose life changes after an unexpected incident. He becomes a half-ghoul and struggles to understand his new identity while trying to live between the human and ghoul worlds.

## Main Characters

- **Ken Kaneki** – The main character who becomes a half-ghoul.
- **Touka Kirishima** – A ghoul who helps Kaneki understand the ghoul world.
- **Hideyoshi Nagachika** – Kaneki's best friend.
  
## Why Tokyo Ghoul Is My Favorite

I like Tokyo Ghoul because it has an interesting storyline, unique characters, and unexpected plot twists. The anime also has an amazing soundtrack and a dark atmosphere that makes every episode exciting.

My favorite things about the anime are:

1. Interesting and unpredictable story.
2. Amazing character development.
3. Unique character designs.
4. Emotional and memorable scenes.
5. Beautiful opening and ending songs.
